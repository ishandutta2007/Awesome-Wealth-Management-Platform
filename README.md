# Awesome-Wealth-Management-Platform

# Top Wealth Management Platform Tools Ecosystem
**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on Portfolio Management, Performance Reporting, Advisor Workstations, Rebalancing, Client Reporting, RIA Technology & Wealth Analytics*
**Last updated: August 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Wealth Management Platforms**. These tools help Registered Investment Advisors (RIAs), family offices, private banks, and wealth managers aggregate portfolio data, calculate performance, generate client reports, rebalance accounts, manage billing, and deliver advisor and client experiences.

**Examples** include Addepar, Black Diamond, Orion Advisor Tech, Tamarac, Nitrogen, Envestnet, Practifi, Wealthbox, AssetBook, AdvisorEngine, and InvestCloud (the category leaders and widely used platforms).

**Open-source emphasis**: Full-featured open-source equivalents to enterprise wealth platforms (with custodial integrations, multi-custodian aggregation, and regulated reporting) remain limited. This section prioritizes the strongest available open-source portfolio management, wealth tracking, and advisor-oriented platforms that teams can self-host or adapt.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites / GitHub repos.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms
- **[Addepar](https://addepar.com/)**  
  Leading portfolio analytics and data platform for complex wealth, family offices, and institutions — strong in alternative assets, look-through reporting, and multi-entity structures.
- **[Black Diamond](https://www.ssctech.com/)** (SS&C)  
  Comprehensive portfolio management, reporting, billing, and client portal solution widely used by RIAs and wealth managers.
- **[Orion Advisor Tech](https://www.orion.com/)**  
  End-to-end advisor platform covering portfolio management, rebalancing, billing, reporting, CRM integrations, and client experience tools.
- **[Tamarac](https://www.envestnet.com/)** (Envestnet)  
  Portfolio management, rebalancing (iRebal), performance reporting, and advisor workstation capabilities within the Envestnet ecosystem.
- **[Nitrogen](https://www.nitrogenwealth.com/)** (formerly Riskalyze)  
  Risk alignment, proposal generation, and client engagement platform focused on quantifying and communicating portfolio risk.
- **[Envestnet](https://www.envestnet.com/)**  
  Broad wealth management technology and managed account platform serving advisors and institutions.
- **[Practifi](https://www.practifi.com/)**  
  CRM and practice management platform designed specifically for wealth management and financial advisory firms.
- **[Wealthbox](https://www.wealthbox.com/)**  
  Modern CRM built for financial advisors, with strong workflow, compliance, and integration capabilities.
- **[AssetBook](https://www.assetbook.com/)**  
  Portfolio management and reporting solution used by advisors and wealth platforms.
- **[AdvisorEngine](https://www.advisorengine.com/)**  
  Digital wealth and advisor technology platform offering client experience, portfolio, and practice tools.
- **[InvestCloud](https://www.investcloud.com/)**  
  Digital wealth platform providing modular solutions for advisors, banks, and asset managers.
- **[Advyzon](https://www.advyzon.com/)**, **[Redtail](https://www.redtailtechnology.com/)**, and related advisor platforms  
  Additional popular tools covering CRM, portfolio, and practice management for RIAs.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects
- **[Finmars](https://github.com/finmars-platform/finmars-core)**  
  Open-source finance and wealth management platform for tracking investments, generating reports and dashboards, and managing portfolios. Self-hostable with a modular marketplace approach.
- **[Wealthbot](https://github.com/wealthbot-io/wealthbot)**  
  Open-source wealth management platform designed to help advisors serve clients, build robo-advisor capabilities, manage portfolios, rebalance, and handle multi-RIA setups.
- **[LibreFolio](https://github.com/Librefolio/LibreFolio)**  
  Self-hosted, open-source financial portfolio tracker for investments, cash, and loans across multiple brokers — with transparent cost-basis and performance calculations.
- **Portfolio Analysis Platforms** (e.g., Pinnacle and similar projects)  
  Open-source systems for portfolio CRUD, performance analysis, stock research, and optimization recommendations.
- **Advisor Desktop / Workstation Prototypes**  
  Open-source or prototype AI-native advisor workstations that demonstrate modern portfolio, client, compliance, and practice workflows.
- **Robo-Advisory & Portfolio Generation Projects**  
  Open-source implementations of mean-variance optimization, Black-Litterman, risk-based portfolio construction, and paper-trading simulation.
- **Personal & Multi-Broker Portfolio Trackers**  
  Community projects focused on aggregating holdings, calculating returns (TWR/MWR), and providing private, self-hosted dashboards.
- **Investment Portfolio Rails / Full-Stack Trackers**  
  Various open-source applications for multi-asset portfolio tracking with market data integration and AI-assisted insights.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- Market data and pricing libraries that feed portfolio systems.
- Performance calculation engines (TWR, MWR, IRR) implemented in open code.
- Document and client portal building blocks for advisor-client collaboration.
- Risk analytics and optimization notebooks (Python/R) that can be embedded in custom platforms.
- Integration patterns with open custodial data feeds or CSV/statement importers.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Smaller firms or family offices sometimes combine **Finmars** or **Wealthbot** as a core with open portfolio trackers and custom reporting. Most production RIA stacks still rely on commercial platforms for custodial connectivity, regulated performance reporting, and billing. Open-source tools excel at private portfolio tracking, research, and prototyping modern advisor experiences.

## Disclaimer
- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Wealth management platforms should be evaluated for custodial aggregation breadth, performance calculation methodology, reporting quality, rebalancing capabilities, billing accuracy, security/compliance posture, and total cost of ownership (often AUM- or account-based).
- Open-source portfolio and wealth tools do not automatically provide the regulated reporting, multi-custodian feeds, or enterprise support of commercial RIA platforms. Always validate suitability for client-facing or regulatory use cases.
